# KORTEX Permit360 — Vision

AI-powered planning, licensing and building intelligence platform. It grows out of Permit Review AI and is built
for Israel first. Other countries are added later as new jurisdiction-specific regulatory layers.

## Why

Permit review today is reactive. Problems are found after submission, the same checks are repeated on every revision,
and the knowledge each committee holds lives in documents and in people. Permit360 moves the work earlier
(prevention), avoids repeating it (incremental execution), and keeps every conclusion traceable to a source and a
measurement.

## Principles (binding on every module)

1. Prevent rather than detect: give architects the same checks before submission.
2. Calculate rather than guess: geometry and numbers are deterministic, and no LLM token is spent on arithmetic.
3. Process only what changed: the Permit Dependency Graph decides what to recompute.
4. Learn once, reuse everywhere: national knowledge is shared, local knowledge is isolated, and verified results are
   reused.
5. Explain every finding: each finding carries its measured value, the permitted value, the method, the drawing
   reference, the source and the rule version.
6. Use AI only where it pays: classification, interpretation and language, measured against cost.
7. Keep professional and legal accountability: no automated approval, and every binding act is human and signed.
8. Minimise operating cost: local models first, cache by fingerprint.
9. Full traceability: hash-chained audit, immutable sources and versioned rules.
10. Integrate rather than replace: municipal systems stay the systems of record.

| Review360 | evidence-based engineering and regulatory review | today's Permit Review AI (IMPLEMENTED core) |

| Construction360 | post-permit construction lifecycle | PROPOSED (phase 4) |

All six share the Kortex Intelligence Engine: geometry, rules, AI gateway, GIS, knowledge and the Permit Dependency
Graph.

## Competitive advantage

The advantage comes from engineering intelligence (measured geometry), regulatory intelligence (executable, versioned
rules with sources), process intelligence, incremental execution and verified knowledge reuse. Access to a model is
not the advantage, since any competitor can buy that.

## What success means (targets, not achievements)

The targets are those in the master prompt §20: 70–90 % less technical review time, 60–80 % less repeated validation,
50–70 % fewer incomplete applications, 60–80 % less avoidable AI cost, 100 % traceability and zero unauthorised
approvals. Baselines are recorded in PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARKS.md. Each target is reported only from measurement.
